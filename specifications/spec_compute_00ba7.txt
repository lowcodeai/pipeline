To capture the compute and tooling specification for your AI pipeline, we need to consider several key aspects, including the computational resources required, the tools and frameworks for data processing and model training, and the deployment environment. Here are some guiding questions to help you gather the necessary information:

### 1. **Compute Resources**
   - **What is the expected workload?** 
     - How many images will you process at once (batch size)?
     - What is the expected training time for the model?
   - **What type of hardware do you have access to?**
     - Do you have access to GPUs or TPUs for training?
     - What is the available RAM and storage capacity?
   - **What is the expected scale of the project?**
     - Will you need to scale up for larger datasets in the future?

### 2. **Data Processing**
   - **What tools will you use for data preprocessing?**
     - Are you familiar with libraries like OpenCV or PIL for image processing?
     - Will you need to augment your dataset? If so, what techniques will you use?
   - **How will you handle DICOM files?**
     - Do you have a library in mind for reading and processing DICOM images (e.g., pydicom)?

### 3. **Model Development**
   - **What frameworks will you use for model training?**
     - Are you considering TensorFlow, PyTorch, or another framework?
   - **What type of model architecture do you plan to use?**
     - Will you use a pre-trained model (transfer learning) or build one from scratch?
   - **How will you manage model training and hyperparameter tuning?**
     - Will you use tools like Keras Tuner or Optuna for hyperparameter optimization?

### 4. **Evaluation and Monitoring**
   - **How will you evaluate the model's performance?**
     - What tools will you use for tracking metrics and visualizing results (e.g., TensorBoard, MLflow)?
   - **How will you handle model versioning and tracking experiments?**
     - Are you planning to use tools like DVC or MLflow for experiment tracking?

### 5. **Deployment**
   - **How do you plan to deploy the model?**
     - Will it be a web application, a mobile app, or an API?
   - **What environment will you use for deployment?**
     - Are you considering cloud services (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) or on-premises solutions?
   - **How will you ensure compliance with privacy and legal regulations during deployment?**

### 6. **Collaboration and Documentation**
   - **What tools will you use for version control and collaboration?**
     - Are you using Git or another version control system?
   - **How will you document your code and processes?**
     - Will you use Jupyter Notebooks, Markdown files, or another method for documentation?

### 7. **Budget and Resources**
   - **What is your budget for computational resources and tools?**
   - **Are there any specific licenses or subscriptions you need to consider?**
