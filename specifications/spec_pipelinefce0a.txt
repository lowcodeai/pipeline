### Human-oriented Outline

1. **Problem Summary & Assumptions**
   - The goal is to develop an AI system that can classify MRI scans for the presence or absence of brain tumors. This will enhance diagnostic accuracy and efficiency for radiologists and researchers. The dataset consists of 1,500 labeled MRI scans, which are well-organized and free of known issues. The data must be anonymized and compliant with healthcare regulations.

2. **Data Specification**
   - The data type is MRI scans stored in a research repository. The key variable is the label indicating tumor presence. The dataset size is 1,500 images, all labeled correctly. The labels indicate whether a tumor is present or absent.

3. **Paths**
   - The project will utilize the following paths:
     - Training Data: `/data/brain_mri/train`
     - Validation Data: `/data/brain_mri/val`
     - Test Data: `/data/brain_mri/test`
     - Labels Location: `/data/brain_mri/labels`
     - Outputs Root: `/home/user/brain_mri_outputs`
     - Checkpoints Directory: `/home/user/brain_mri_outputs/checkpoints`
     - Logs Directory: `/home/user/brain_mri_outputs/logs`

4. **Preprocessing & Feature Engineering**
   - The MRI images will be preprocessed to ensure they are in a consistent format and size. This may include resizing the images, normalizing pixel values, and augmenting the dataset with techniques such as rotation, flipping, and zooming to improve model robustness. Anonymization will also be applied to ensure compliance with healthcare regulations.

5. **Model Selection**
   - A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) will be selected for this binary classification task due to its effectiveness in image classification tasks. A pre-trained model such as ResNet50 or EfficientNet will be fine-tuned on the MRI dataset to leverage transfer learning, which can improve performance given the relatively small dataset size.

6. **Training Configuration**
   - The training will use a batch size of 16, with the Adam optimizer and a learning rate of 0.001. The model will be trained for 50 epochs, with early stopping based on validation loss to prevent overfitting. Checkpoints will be saved in the specified checkpoints directory.

7. **Evaluation Plan**
   - The model's performance will be evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score on the test dataset. A confusion matrix will also be generated to visualize the classification results. The evaluation metrics will help assess the model's effectiveness in distinguishing between tumor-present and tumor-absent cases.
